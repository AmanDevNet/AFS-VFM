======================================================================
CONCLUSIONS FOR PAPER
======================================================================

1. Vision foundation models exhibit PREDICTABLE failure patterns
   under systematic visual degradation.

2. Internal representations (embeddings, logits) show measurable
   drift 2-5 frames BEFORE the final prediction changes.

3. Self-supervised training (DINOv2) produces more robust features
   than contrastive (CLIP) or supervised (DETR) training.

4. Contrastive models exhibit sharp logit margin collapse,
   yielding high failure anticipation capability.

5. Model ensembles can significantly reduce failure rates,
   as different architectures fail on different images.

6. These findings provide empirical ground for deploying early
   warning monitors in safety-critical autonomous vision stacks.
